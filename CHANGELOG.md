# Changelog

## 0.0.10 — keyboard-stable expression controls, 30 October 2026

Keep meaning, binding-copy and braille controls focusable during asynchronous
work, with guarded repeat activation and generation-bound result updates. Give
each control an expression-specific accessible name and expose related results
through controls/expanded relationships. A brief persistent status announces
availability without placing mathematical transcripts, YAML or braille cells in
a live region. Ordinary completion leaves the reader's current focus alone; a
failed export returns only its own remaining focus before its button is hidden.

Wrap long control labels and compact the related-proof navigation without
removing native proof numbering, authored labels or mathematical content.
Native/browser and assistive-technology acceptance remain separate from pure
control-state checks. Material AI assistance and its limits remain documented.

## 0.0.9 — explicit intent meanings and current render controls, 28 October 2026

Local development candidate; no push or release. Extend the finite English
intent vocabulary to 19 exact concepts with declared arity/operand order and
a deliberate fixity subset. Keep recursive interpretation, whole-expression
fallback and the original MathML, ARIA and presentation-based braille separate.

Refresh owned controls after subsequent MathJax passes, remove retired controls,
avoid recycled expression IDs and invalidate changed source bindings. Pure/VM
checks passed. A full regression passed before a narrow repair for a real
MathJax-added invisible application operator. Keep raw checkpoint text, exact
authored operands and strict checks on preexisting markers. Fresh affected
pure checks, two native QMD pages, three combined targets and four actual
MathJax browser groups passed after the repair. All 26 native-page transcript
controls were exercised: 25 finite interpretations and one expected complete
native fallback. The complete full-suite result remains tied to its pre-repair
source. Speech meaning, human AT and wider conformance remain separate.

Scope semantic-URI registration to native mathematical blocks and explicit AM
authoring/association markers. Generic foreign language realizations may share a
canonical URI. Keep native fragment IDs unique and preserve owned mathematical
identity refusals, even when foreign classes are also present. The main filter
and native catalogue API use one ownership predicate; exact companion API pins
must match this candidate.
